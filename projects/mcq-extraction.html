01 / Overview

Make question creation from long study documents faster.

The content team needed to turn large PDFs into exam-style questions. Manual transcription slowed that work, while long uploads needed to run without blocking the application.

I built a FastAPI service that connects PDF processing with Google GenAI and returns extracted multiple-choice questions. The workflow was used across 5+ clients and supported more than 1,000 users.

02 / Approach

Measure extraction quality and keep long jobs moving.

  • Tested and refined the workflow across 100+ large PDFs.
  • Improved extraction accuracy from an initial 40% to 99.5% through iterative refinement.
  • Implemented asynchronous background processing for large uploads and real-time processing-status updates.
  • Deployed and maintained the service, including server setup, monitoring, debugging, and production upkeep.

03 / Impact
