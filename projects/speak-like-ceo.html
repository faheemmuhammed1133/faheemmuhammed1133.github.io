01 / Overview

Make speech practice a guided habit, not a one-time script.

Speak Like CEO explores how generative AI can help people prepare and deliver a speech. The product concept pairs generated speech with a repeatable practice routine, guiding learners from hearing a model to presenting in their own words.

The experience was planned for children, students, and professionals, with feedback as part of the learning loop.

02 / Approach

Organize learning around five clear stages.

The planned workflow combines LLM speech generation, text-to-speech, guided repetition, recall, and a final performance.
