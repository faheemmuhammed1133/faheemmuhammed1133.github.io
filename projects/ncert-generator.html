01 / Overview

Help students review textbook chapters in their language.

The project starts with the source material: retrieve textbook chapters in multiple languages, preserve the extracted content, then use GenAI APIs to produce study-friendly outputs.

It covers English, Hindi, Tamil, Arabic, Malayalam, and Urdu, with chapter summaries, key-topic breakdowns, and likely examination questions as outputs.

02 / Approach

Keep textbook retrieval and generated study aids connected.

  • Built a pipeline to retrieve NCERT and Indian school textbook chapters in six languages.
  • Checked extraction accuracy against the tested textbook content.
  • Used GenAI APIs to produce summaries, key topics, and likely exam questions from extracted chapter content.

03 / Outcome

One multilingual pipeline, three study formats.
