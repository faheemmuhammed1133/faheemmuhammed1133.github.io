01 / Overview

Give English learners a place to practice speaking out loud.

SpeekiTalk AI is a spoken-English conversational application built to help learners practice through adaptive conversations and receive feedback on their speech. The experience combines conversation, live transcription, and an automated feedback step.

I developed the application as part of my AI / Generative AI Developer internship at Trogon Media.

02 / Approach

Connect conversational AI with speech services.

  • Integrated OpenAI GPT-3.5, Deepgram Nova-3, and Azure APIs into the spoken-English experience.
  • Designed the product flow around adaptive conversations, live transcription, and automated speech feedback.
  • Worked across the service lifecycle, from API integration to deployment and ongoing maintenance.
